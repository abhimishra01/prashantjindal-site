Oatly: supply chain transformation

  1. Diagnosed Oatly as stuck between two supply chain models.
  2. Mapped a 5-year roadmap to move the company from reactive to resilient: cloud ERP, outside-in demand sensing and modular manufacturing.

Sport Obermeyer: operations and forecasting

  1. The case: USD 5M of production commitments, made 4 months before demand data was available, for a skiwear line with high uncertainty.
  2. Restructured the commitments with fabric postponement and risk based sourcing: Hong Kong for uncertain styles, China for predictable classics.
  3. The aim was lower inventory exposure.

Patagonia: digital innovation

  1. Devised LoopID, a digital product passport that uses an NFC chip and blockchain.
  2. LoopID is designed to cut resale processing from 15 minutes to under 2, at under USD 1 per unit.
  3. It is also designed to put Patagonia ahead of the incoming EU Digital Product Passport regulation.

Aqualia: CSRD materiality

  1. Assessed double materiality against a significance threshold of 3.5 out of 5.
  2. Found the ESG topics most material to Aqualia's 2027 to 2030 strategy, ahead of mandatory CSRD reporting.
  • Corporate Finance: DCF, WACC, LBO
  • Applied Data Analytics
  • Six Sigma, DMAIC
  • Digital Business Strategy
  • Supply chain
  • ESG and CSRD
